# Cofounder Match

## Your AI agent for finding the person worth building with

Finding a cofounder is a high-stakes compatibility decision, but existing products mostly offer profiles, filters, and cold introductions. They optimize discovery rather than the working relationship that has to survive pressure.

Cofounder Match replaces browsing with an agent. It interviews each founder, understands what they can own and where they need leverage, searches the pool, and brings back one person worth meeting.

## How it works

1. **Founder interview** — Capture skills, proof, ambition, availability, values, working style, and dealbreakers through conversation.
2. **Living founder brief** — Model what the founder brings, what is missing, and which partnership is likely to work.
3. **Private matching harness** — Specialist agents analyze complementarity, commitment, trust, communication, motivation, and practical fit.
4. **One considered match** — Explain why the pairing could work, where it could fail, and what both people should explore.
5. **Trial sprint** — Coordinate an introduction and propose a small real project before either person commits.
6. **Feedback loop** — Learn from both founders and either help formalize the partnership or improve the next match.

## Why it wins

- Outcomes instead of profile browsing
- Compatibility beyond résumé keywords
- One high-confidence introduction instead of endless options
- Real work together before commitment
- Private matching, scoring, and agent orchestration
- Scheduling and follow-through handled by the product

## Initial market

Start with ambitious university founders, student builders, and technical talent looking for complementary cofounders. Expand into accelerators, startup communities, research commercialization, and experienced operators entering entrepreneurship.

## North-star metric

The percentage of matched pairs that complete a trial sprint and choose to continue building together.
